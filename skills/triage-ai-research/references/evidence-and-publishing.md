# Evidence and publishing boundary

Evidence states are ordered: discovered, read, reproduced, applied, verified. Never collapse them. An abstract supports discovery; a controlled local intervention supports an adoption claim.

For an implementation recommendation, define a hypothesized mechanism, a fixed baseline, one intervention, task-family holdouts, outcome and cost metrics, and an acceptance gate. Keep model, permissions, tools, and evaluator constant where possible.

For a public draft, include the problem, what the paper actually demonstrates, evaluation conditions, limitations, one concrete experiment, the exact arXiv version, and an explicit status: `not reproduced`, `reproduced`, or `applied locally`. Publishing remains human-approved.
